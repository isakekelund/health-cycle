How scoring works
Each habit done = +1 point. Each miss in a row doubles — 1st miss = −1, 2nd = −2, 3rd = −4, 4th = −7, then it stays at −7 until you do it again.
A day counts as positive when total points are above 0.
A day counts as negative when total points are 0 or below.
Only Monday–Thursday are tracked. Goal: +4 per week.

We live such unnatural lifestyles today that staying healthy now takes an unnatural number of deliberate actions — things a body a few generations ago never had to think about. Sleep, movement, food, light, screens, recovery — all of it now needs conscious effort just to counteract the environment we've built.

Nobody can hold all of that in their head, every day, forever. That's the whole point of this app: not to gamify your life, but to offload the remembering, so you can just do the thing.
